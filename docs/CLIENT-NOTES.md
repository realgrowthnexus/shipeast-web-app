# Client notes: what Derion asked for

Read from the full WhatsApp history (April 14 to November 12, 2026), the proposal, scope, quote
and checklist PDFs, his two policy PDFs (August 22), his policy and content brief (November 3)
and his PriceSmart price list. Written November 13, 2026. No passwords or keys belong in this
file: the chat contains several, and he was told to change them.

## What he wants

A website at shipeastja.com where a Jamaican overseas buys groceries and essentials for
family already in Jamaica. A ShipEast driver buys the items locally (mainly PriceSmart) and
delivers to the door. **Nothing is shipped; no customs.** He corrected this twice (May 14,
July 29), so it must be unmistakable on the site.

- Model to beat: **grocerylistjamaica.com** (resells PriceSmart at a markup plus a service fee).
- Layout he pointed at: **upercart.com** (September 20): a storefront with banners, category
  tiles with product pictures, and product rails with prices.
- Local competitor: **revvcouriers.com** (St. Thomas, launched October 10; red and yellow).
- He wants to **edit supermarket prices himself** in the admin panel (November 12).

## The deal

Quote: USD$1,500 fixed for the launch system; five add-ons (USD$600) not confirmed. Paid so
far: USD$350. Budget is tight; he pays in small parts. The Flutter delivery app is a separate,
finished project.

- Red is the brand. "My RED should stand out, the white just looks regular." Plain red on
  white is "not premium"; he likes "red mixed with a little black" and a glossy feel.
- Early font wish for the app: Montserrat Black headings, Nunito Black buttons, Inter body,
  Dancing Script accents. So: heavy, rounded, friendly, with a script accent.
- Company name: ShipEast Couriers & Bearer Service Ltd ("Service", singular).
- `J$3,100`, `USD$31`, `July 31, 2026`, `1-876-123-1234`, Title Case names, "Groceries".
- IDs: `SE-CUS-XXXXXX`, `SE-DRV-XXXXXX`, never sequential; orders `SE-10428`.
- "Delivering to St. Thomas, Jamaica" or "Kingston, Jamaica" until they expand.
- Statuses are specific; "Pending" is only an admin filter. Never show placeholder numbers.

1. Prices: his list is in J$ (PriceSmart price, GCT, ShipEast price); the app stores US cents
   with a placeholder rate. Markup not final.
2. Catalogue: about 63 grocery items in ten grocery categories (he says about 90 coming); the
   app has 22 demo products in four departments.
3. Product photos: his sheet embeds 57 PriceSmart catalogue images. Our rule is not to use
   PriceSmart's images; the owner has to decide.
4. Same-day: his November 3 brief says not to show a guaranteed same-day promise.
5. Substitution default: "Ask me first".
6. Policies: his texts are not on the legal pages yet; the August PDFs were written for the
   mobile app (cash on delivery, merchants) and need adapting.
7. Scope creep in his copy: "Book a delivery" and orders from people living in Jamaica.
