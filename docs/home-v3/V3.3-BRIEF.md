# Home v3.3: the owner's third review and the manager's rulings (November 13, 2026)

Home page only. Every other page stays as it is (BRAIN §7, "save credits").

## 1. What the owner said (translated from his voice note)

Better than before, but not perfect yet. "Act as the expert designer and developer; you decide."

1. **Header.** The main row (logo, search, account, basket) is cramped: it needs real padding above
   and below. The bar under it (the aisles) needs padding too. The whole header feels too tight.
2. **Hero.** Sizes are fine; the inner padding is wrong. The crimson panel "doesn't look good".
   The three cards under it need better padding and should be smaller. One big main panel and three
   small ones, organised as one balanced group.
3. **Departments.** The arch photo frame is wrong. The photo must fill its frame.
4. **Staples shelf.** The feel is off: button and inner padding, and too much roundness, above all
   on the images.
5. **Ready-made baskets.** Nice. Tune the padding if needed.
6. **Gift banner** ("Happy birthday, Grandma"). A big padding problem.
7. **"Send groceries, not money."** Good, but give it more room and a much bigger headline. "Small"
   never meant "make everything small". A designer decides where a heading should be big.
8. **Green Jamaica band.** Almost fine.
9. **FAQ.** Opens with a jerky, "sticky" jump. Make it smooth with Motion (Framer Motion), and give
   the whole page better motion.
10. Run expert design auditors to find what the page lacks. The manager makes the decisions.

## 2. The audits

Three auditors measured the page with Playwright at 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 px, with both design
skills loaded:

- `docs/home-v3/audits/layout.md`: spacing, proportion, radius and type hierarchy.
- `docs/home-v3/audits/motion.md`: the FAQ, and motion across the page.
- `docs/home-v3/audits/gaps.md`: what is missing, judged against the competitors.

The finding behind the owner's feeling: the gaps **between** sections are already even (40/48px).
The cramped look comes from **inside** the boxes: 0px padding on the aisle row, 10px around the
header controls, boxes nested three deep, 32–40px corners on almost every block (above the
24px ceiling in CLAUDE.md), and a flat type scale where nothing below the hero exceeds 27px.

## 3. Rulings (these are the spec; the plan implements exactly these)

**Accepted from the layout audit** (values in `audits/layout.md` §2–§3):

- R1 radius scale: blocks 24px (`rounded-2xl`), cards 16px (`rounded-xl`), product cards 12px
  (`rounded-lg`), images in cards and every button and input 8px (`rounded-md`). Pills only for
  information (stickers, price tags, chips, lane labels, the ticker). No `rounded-3xl` or
  `rounded-4xl` anywhere on the home page.
- R2 rhythm: plain sections stay 40/48px apart; title to content 24px; bands get real inner air
  (proof `section`, Jamaica and closing `section-sm`).
- R3 type: one BIG moment, the proof headline in `text-display` at weight 600 (48 → 77px). Hero
  `text-h1` unchanged. Jamaica and closing headlines move up to `text-h2`. New size role
  `--text-price` (18px) for shelf prices and basket price stickers.
- Header 3.1, hero 3.2, departments 3.3, shelf 3.4, baskets and gift block 3.5, proof 3.6, steps,
  Jamaica and closing 3.7–3.9: all as written there.

**Accepted from the motion audit** (`audits/motion.md`): the new home FAQ accordion (§1), rows 1–12
of the page-wide table (§2), the tokens (§3). The shared `ui/accordion.tsx` stays untouched for `/faq`.

**Accepted from the gaps audit** (`audits/gaps.md`): 1 (WhatsApp and phone 876-797-4301, plus a
"WhatsApp us" strip link), 2 (featured baskets drop unavailable ones and top up to three), 3 (one
exchange-rate line), 4 (FAQ answer: "We ask you first"), 5 (one sentence on who we are; the brief
confirms the company is based in St. Thomas), 6 (no item counts on the home tiles), 7 (ticker pause
button), 8 (phone cookie banner at most 120px tall), 9 (search border `border-line-strong`), 10
(photo first on phones), 11 (footer links 44px; social handles as plain text links), 12 (the hero
tile is renamed "Baskets ready to go"; section titles take no full stop).

**Rejected or deferred, with reasons:**

- Adding "ask you first" to the proof checklist: the checklist is removed (the captions carry the
  proof); the FAQ answer carries it.
- Payment line in the hero (gaps 13): the hero ticks are removed for room; "Pay in US dollars" is
  in the ticker and step 2.
- Quantity stepper, aisle tabs on the shelf (gaps, upercart patterns): next round, with the shop page.
- Basket photo re-crops (gaps 14): only the vegetable tile's offset changes; new photos wait.
- The dev-only "2 Issues" overlay is a Next.js framework bug (gaps 15). We only confirm the build
